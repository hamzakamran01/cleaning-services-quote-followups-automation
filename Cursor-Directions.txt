This is CleanProposal AI — a sales automation tool built specifically for commercial
cleaning company owners. The user is a cleaning business owner (Sarah Chen, Sales Owner).
Their world is: clean white spaces, precision, reliability, the satisfaction of a
perfectly spotless surface. The visual language should reflect that world.

Design identity:
- Color system: Warm near-white base (#F8F7F4), deep slate primary (#1A1D23),
  electric teal accent (#00C5A1), warm amber for hot/urgency signals (#F59E0B),
  emerald for wins/success (#10B981), soft rose for warnings (#F43F5E).
- Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans for all headings and display numbers (bold, tight
  letter-spacing). Inter for all body text, labels, table cells.
- Aesthetic: Premium B2B SaaS. Think Linear meets Stripe. Clean surfaces, generous
  whitespace, subtle borders (1px, 8% opacity). No rounded-everything — cards use
  8px radius, inputs use 6px, pills use 999px. Shadows are paper-thin (0 1px 3px
  rgba(0,0,0,0.06)).
- Micro-interactions: Framer Motion for page transitions (fade + 4px Y shift, 200ms),
  card hover (shadow lift), kanban drag-drop, stat counter animations on mount.
- Status color system (apply consistently everywhere):
    Draft     → slate bg  (#F1F1F0), slate text (#64748B)
    Sent      → blue bg   (#EFF6FF), blue text  (#3B82F6)
    Opened    → purple bg (#F5F3FF), purple text (#7C3AED)
    Hot Lead  → amber bg  (#FFFBEB), amber text  (#D97706) + 🔥 icon
    Won       → emerald bg (#F0FDF4), emerald text (#059669)
    Lost      → rose bg   (#FFF1F2), rose text   (#F43F5E)
